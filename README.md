# vesper

<p align="center">
  <img src="./assets/readme/hero.jpg" width="100%" alt="Vesper: daily briefing generator — aggregates signals into concise morning and evening briefings">
</p>

Vesper aggregates signals from every other skill and assembles them into morning or evening briefings in natural language. Routine background activity, speculative observations, and already-experienced events are excluded. Pending decision requests are presented with option, benefit, and cost framing.

**Capabilities:**
- Morning and evening briefing assembly
- Strict signal filtering: no noise, no repeats
- Decision framing with option, benefit, and cost
